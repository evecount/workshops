## Why Code-First Workshops?

- **Zero toy slides:** Every session runs against real, inspectable repositories and reproducible benchmarks.
- **Built for constrained compute:** Heavy emphasis on sparse adapters, frozen backbones, and semantic pruning so teams don't burn GPU budgets unnecessarily.
- **Tailored to your audience:** Configurable for senior ML engineers, product builders, or university cohorts.
